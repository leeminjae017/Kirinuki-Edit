## Caption design - this is where the difference is

**Our videos:** every line uses the same template - large CookieRun 140 + pop-in + thick outline. Only emphasis changes colour.

**Reference channels: captions look different for each job they do.**

### 로션욤 (a very consistent template)
| Use | Look |
|---|---|
| Ordinary dialogue | bold gothic, white text + thin dark outline, **small (about 5% of frame width)**, one line low in the window. The face is the star |
| Stage directions / narration | **parentheses + pale yellow** "(답답해서 마우스를 뺏으신)" "(표독 두꺼비 등장)" - same font |
| Punchline | large, bold, thick outline "ㅋㅋㅋㅋㅋ" "거지야!" "아니야ㅠㅠ" - **rarely, once** |
| Chat quote | white rounded label + **the channel mascot's face** "역시 댄스 동아리 일진 ㅋㅋ" |
| Reaction symbol | yellow "?" + concentric ripples (when puzzled) |
| Top band title | always a white line + a yellow line, two lines |
| Inserts | chibi drawings of her own character, stream meme photos, donation alert captures |

## Why our videos look "mass-produced" (guess - compared with the two channels)

1. **Captions use one template.** The reference channels style dialogue, narration, chat and punchlines differently. Popping
   every line large in one template feels like "auto captions for shorts".
2. **Every line was zoomed on the same beat.** The reference channels leave the cam alone and add something only when needed.
3. **Cards are the common black rectangle + italics.** The reference labels carry the character's colour or mascot - they
   belong to that channel.
4. **Sources have nothing to do with the character** (いらすとや, product photos). The reference channels use things from the
   stream (chat, donations, game screen) plus drawings of their own character.
5. **Colours do not come from the character.** Yellow and black are everywhere. 정타비 fixes Tabi's sky blue and 로션욤 yellow
   as the channel colour.

## Direction for Damyui (guess; to be tried on the 모캠 밀림 copy)

- **Colours from Damyui:** pastel blue (#6FCFF5) and white are the channel colours. Chat pill outlines, the title key word,
  reaction symbols.
- **Split captions by use:** dialogue (current template) / stage directions in parentheses (pale yellow, small) / chat pill
  (double outline in Damyui's colours).
- **Zoom only at emotional moments:** one or two slow zooms on beats like sulking or feeling wronged.
- **One punchline:** window only in black and white + one line of narration.
- **Sources from the stream:** captures of the original chat area as Damyui-coloured pills.
