- **"Change"** = a moment where the colour distribution or brightness inside the window (title band excluded) changes a
  lot (changes within 0.3 s count once). The threshold is eyeballed, so read the differences between channels rather
  than the absolute values.
- **Slow zooms (Ken Burns) are rare.** Of shots held longer than 0.8 s, 0-25% change size by more than 3% (mostly
  0-10%). The energy comes **from the source inside the window changing often**, not from camera moves.
- Zoom and shake (frame-to-frame transforms) are almost zero in all 20 videos - no screen-shake effects.
- **Sound (not verified):** loud moments at 0.45-1.35 per second, similar to 삼성 (1.28); the noise floor at -33 to -57 dB
  overlaps 삼성 (-36). This method cannot separate sound effects or music from speech - it has to be listened to.

## Grammar shared by all four channels (observed)

Images: `img/reps.jpg` (one per channel), `img/sheet_*.jpg` (5 videos x 8 frames), `img/strip_*.jpg` (1.5 s around a
change, 10 fps).

1. **Letterbox frame.** Black bands top and bottom, video window in the middle. Measured window position: top band 15-22%
   of the frame height, bottom band 5-23%.
2. **Top band = a hook title shown for the whole video.** One or two lines, bold gothic, white text with **only the key
   word coloured** (yellow most often; 정타비 uses orange, red, blue, pink). E.g. "치지직 **감쓰통** VS 치지직 **변기통**".
3. **Bottom band = channel mark.** 미도미도 마요 uses a logo, 정타비 "아라하시 타비 / 25/11/19 [타비 마술쇼]" (stream name and
   date); 형독 and 라코코 leave it empty.
4. **Captions sit low inside the window** and change with speech (same as Damyui now). Each channel has a distinct font:
   - 라코코: large, heavy brush-like font, thick black outline, two lines, red for emphasis
   - 정타비: rounded handwriting font, white text + dark outline, name tags "(흑돌)" "(백돌)" when two people speak
   - 형독: black text on white boxes (labels), yellow / blue for emphasised words
5. **The source inside the window changes about every second.** Kinds of change (most frequent first):
   - **straight cut** to another scene (stream screen, game, the 3D model from another angle)
   - **tight face crop** (only the face, large, at a reaction)
   - **inserted screens**: chat, donation or community post captures, cam view (PiP), real photos
   - **stickers and cut-out memes** (Pepe, a finger photo), **particle effects** (라코코's green sparkles)
   - **graphics**: a moving **focus bracket [ ]** (정타비, gomoku), **process diagrams** with arrows and names (정타비),
     **speech balloons** (형독, 미도미도), **chapter titles** "0. 사건의 시작 / 1. 부정 / 2. 분노" (라코코)
   - **text cards**: black text on white explaining the situation, then a cut to video (형독)
6. **How things come in.** Mostly straight cuts. Inserted images **grow in** from a small window to fill the window
   (정타비 process diagram, about 0.8 s); focus brackets **slide** to their new spot. No screen shake or flashy transitions
   were seen.

## Differences per channel

- **형독** - live-action photos and video, face compositing (a head on another body), comic speech balloons, cut-out people
  on background photos. As the user said, it creates new sources.
- **미도미도 마요** - mostly hand-drawn character illustrations (mascot, cross sections, many expressions). Copying it means
  drawing new art.
- **라코코** - rapid sequences of the 3D model from changing angles and distances, layered with stream captures, cam and chat.
  Chapter titles split the story. **The most frequent changes of the four.**
- **정타비** - uses the stream footage as is but points out "where to look now" with crops, focus brackets, diagrams and meme
  stickers. The bottom band names the stream.

## Compared with Damyui's 삼성

삼성 now shows one shot of the Damyui cam at the same size for all 38 s on the preset background, and only the captions
change (picture motion 0.4). In the reference channels **something besides the captions changes inside the window about
every second.**

- **Pace target (guess):** for a 38 s video, 라코코's pace means 30-55 changes and 정타비's 12-30. Somewhere between, **25-40
  changes**, looks like a safe first attempt.
- **The 형독 and 미도미도 마요 approach (drawing or compositing new sources)** is hard to follow, as the user said. The closest
  is putting a cut-out face on another background.
- **Reusable source library:** focus brackets, arrows, speech balloons, chapter title cards and emphasis stickers can be made
  once, kept in one place and reused in other projects. Internet images (product photos, logos, memes) raise **copyright and
  trademark** questions - record the source and terms of use when downloading.

## Limits (not verified)

- Downloaded at 360p, so caption font names and exact colours could not be measured (visual impression only).
- Only 8 frames per video plus 6 strips around changes were viewed - not all 20 videos from start to end.
- The thresholds for changes and slow zooms are eyeballed.
- Sound effects and music could not be separated numerically - they have to be listened to.
